        How to Use HomeCast

        Quick Start

        1. Pick a Chromecast, AirPlay device or LG TV from the device list
        2. Paste a video URL or a webpage containing a video (it's analyzed straight away)
        3. Click "Start Casting" to begin playback
        4. Pause, skip and change the volume from the dashboard

        Tips

        • Proxy stream (under Advanced): Keep this on for most videos. It fixes CORS errors and enables HLS/m3u8 streaming.
        • Up next: While a stream plays, "Queue a video" adds another for the same device. It starts when the current one ends, even with this page closed. On Apple TV, press "Play next now" instead.
        • Recent: Videos you've cast are listed under the URL field. Click one to analyze it again.
        • Stopping: Stop and the × on a stream ask you to click again to confirm, so a stray click doesn't end playback.
        • Subtitles: Subtitles found with a video can be picked before casting, or add a WebVTT/SRT file by URL. On a Chromecast you can switch them or turn them off while playing; on Apple TV, use the TV remote.
        • Manual IP: If your device isn't found, choose "Enter IP address…" at the bottom of the device list and type its IP address.
        • Supported Formats: MP4, WebM, HLS (m3u8), and many streaming services.

        Send pages from other tabs

        • Bookmarklet: Drag Cast with HomeCast to your bookmarks bar. On a page with a video, click it: HomeCast opens with the page ready to cast. No bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show it. Or , add a bookmark, and paste it as the bookmark's address.
        • Links and shortcuts: Opening /?url=… with a page's address does the same, for phone shortcuts and scripts.
        • Share menu: Installed as an app over HTTPS, HomeCast appears in your phone's share menu.

        Troubleshooting

        • No devices found: Ensure the device and the server are on the same network, then press the rescan button next to the device list.
        • Video doesn't play: Check that "Proxy stream" under Advanced is on.
        • Connection issues: Check firewall and network settings.
